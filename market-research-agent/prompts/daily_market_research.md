# Daily Market Research

Generate a comprehensive pre-market briefing using the stocks listed in `watchlist.txt`.

## Objectives

Analyze the current market and identify high-quality swing trading opportunities.

## Include

### 1. Market Overview
- Major index futures
- Treasury yields
- VIX
- Dollar Index
- Oil
- Gold

### 2. Macro Events
- Economic reports today
- Federal Reserve speakers
- Important geopolitical developments

### 3. Sector Rotation
Identify sectors showing relative strength and weakness.

### 4. Company Research

For each stock in the watchlist:

- Recent news
- Earnings announcements
- Analyst upgrades or downgrades
- SEC filings (if applicable)
- Technical trend
- Bull thesis
- Bear thesis
- Key price levels
- Overall conviction (High, Medium, Low)

### 5. Highest Conviction Trades

Rank the five best opportunities.

For each include:

- Symbol
- Thesis
- Catalyst
- Biggest risk
- Suggested swing timeframe

### 6. Risks

Summarize today's biggest market risks.

## Output

Use the template located at:

skills/market-research/templates/daily_report.md

Save the completed report to:

reports/latest.md
