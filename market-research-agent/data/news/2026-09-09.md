# Market News — 2026-09-09

- U.S. equities sold off Tuesday after the holiday: S&P 500 -0.6%, Dow -1.2%, Nasdaq -0.3%; Brent briefly approached $99.50 as Middle East conflict disrupted oil flows.
- Wednesday premarket futures were lower with crude near $100, keeping inflation and rate-hike concerns in focus.
- Semiconductor leadership is a key countertrend: AMD rose roughly 6% Tuesday after discussing a potential $2 trillion AI market and data-center sales approaching $70 billion in 2027; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index continued to outperform.
- AI infrastructure remains stronger than software and rate-sensitive growth, but the tape is narrow and vulnerable to profit-taking.
- Kratos disclosed a >$20 million mobile SATCOM gateway award on September 1 and an approximately $35 million national-security hardware award on August 31.
- POET is exhibiting at CIOE 2026 in Shenzhen September 9–11, highlighting photonic integrated circuits and high-power laser sources for AI interconnects.
