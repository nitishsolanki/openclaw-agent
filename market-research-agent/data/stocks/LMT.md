# Lockheed Martin (LMT)

## Overview
Lockheed Martin is one of the largest pure-play defense contractors, with major businesses in Aeronautics, Missiles and Fire Control, Rotary and Mission Systems, and Space. Key programs include F-35, missile defense, tactical missiles, Sikorsky helicopters, satellites, and classified systems.

## Bull thesis
- Lockheed is a central beneficiary of long-cycle US/allied defense priorities: air dominance, missile defense, space, hypersonics, and munitions replenishment.
- The F-35 installed base creates decades of production, sustainment, software, and upgrade revenue.
- Pure-play defense exposure can provide resilient cash flows when commercial cycles weaken.
- Shareholder returns are historically meaningful through dividends and buybacks.

## Bear thesis
- Heavy dependence on US government budgets, contracting cycles, and political priorities.
- F-35 production/sustainment issues, cost scrutiny, or international procurement delays could weigh on growth and margins.
- Fixed-price development programs and classified/space work can create unexpected charges.
- Defense primes may face margin pressure if governments push affordability despite higher demand.

## Risks
- Budget sequestration or continuing resolutions; program cancellations; execution charges; supply-chain and labor shortages; export controls; geopolitical procurement shifts; headline/political risk around defense spending.

## Catalysts
- New F-35 lots and international orders; missile-defense and hypersonic awards; Ukraine/NATO replenishment contracts; space/classified wins; clean earnings beats; capital-return increases.

## Long-term outlook
Lockheed remains a core defense holding with durable franchise assets. Growth may be moderate rather than explosive, but backlog visibility, strategic relevance, and cash returns support a favorable long-term profile.

## Conviction rating
Medium-high conviction: best-in-class defense positioning, tempered by budget/program concentration and valuation sensitivity.
