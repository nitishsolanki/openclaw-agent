# TSM (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company)

_Last updated: 2026-07-26 CDT_

## Overview
TSMC is the world's leading pure-play semiconductor foundry, manufacturing advanced chips for fabless and integrated-device customers. Its core moat is process technology leadership, scale, yield learning, customer trust and advanced packaging capacity.

## Bull thesis
TSMC is the manufacturing backbone of the AI semiconductor cycle. Demand for advanced-node logic, high-bandwidth packaging and HPC chips should support revenue growth and pricing power. Its customer base spans Nvidia, AMD, Apple, Broadcom and many others, making it a broad pick-and-shovel AI beneficiary rather than a single-product bet.

## Bear thesis
The stock's core risk is not technology quality but geopolitics, cyclicality and capital intensity. If AI chip demand slows, customers double-order less, or capex outruns utilization, margins could compress. Overseas fabs may carry higher costs than Taiwan fabs.

## Risks
Taiwan geopolitical risk, earthquake/power/water disruption, export controls, customer concentration, advanced packaging bottlenecks, capex intensity, FX, and cyclicality in non-AI semiconductors.

## Catalysts
Quarterly revenue/guidance beats, AI/HPC demand acceleration, N2 production milestones, advanced packaging expansion, improving smartphone/PC cycles, and evidence overseas fabs are scaling without severe margin drag.

## Long-term outlook
Very positive. TSMC remains the highest-quality foundry franchise globally and should compound with advanced-node demand, though investors must underwrite geopolitical and capital-cycle volatility.

## Conviction rating
High conviction: exceptional business quality and AI leverage, with uniquely large geopolitical tail risk.
