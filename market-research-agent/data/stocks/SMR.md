# SMR — NuScale Power

## Overview
NuScale Power develops small modular reactor (SMR) nuclear technology. Its core product is the NuScale Power Module, aimed at scalable carbon-free baseload power for utilities, industrial customers, and potentially data-center/AI power demand. The company is pre-commercial or early-commercial, so valuation depends more on project pipeline and financing than current earnings.

## Sector / Industry
- Sector: Industrials / Energy Technology
- Industry: Nuclear Technology / Small Modular Reactors

- Retail interest remains high around nuclear power themes, especially grid reliability and AI/data-center electricity demand.
- Company site navigation confirms focus on NuScale Power Module, plant services, strategic partners, SEC filings, quarterly results, and SMR education.

## Bull thesis
- SMRs could solve a real bottleneck: reliable, carbon-free power for grids, industrial loads, and data centers.
- NuScale has brand recognition and regulatory/history advantages versus many pre-commercial nuclear startups.
- Any firm customer order, government support, or partner financing can materially re-rate the stock.

## Bear thesis
- Commercial nuclear projects are slow, capital intensive, regulated, and prone to delays/cost overruns.
- Revenue is currently limited relative to market expectations, making valuation highly speculative.
- Equity raises can extend runway but dilute shareholders.

## Risks
- Licensing delays; project cancellations; financing gaps; cost inflation; political/regulatory changes; technology not reaching economic deployment; customer concentration.

## Catalysts
- Signed power/project agreements; NRC/regulatory milestones; DOE or international support; strategic investment; clearer construction timeline; data-center offtake interest.

## Long-term outlook
NuScale offers leveraged exposure to the SMR theme, but the path to durable cash flow remains uncertain. It is more venture-style than mature industrial investment.

## Conviction rating
Low-Medium conviction.
