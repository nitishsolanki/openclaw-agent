# APP — AppLovin Corporation

**As of:** 2026-08-24  
**Sector / industry:** Communication Services / Advertising Technology  
**Conviction:** Medium — high-quality business, high expectations, elevated execution risk

## Snapshot

APP is a performance-advertising platform built around AppLovin Ads, the MAX mediation platform, Adjust, and Wurl. Its core advantage is Axon, the AI recommendation system that matches ads with likely converters across a large mobile-app audience. AppLovin opened its self-serve advertising platform to all advertisers in June 2026, broadening the potential customer base beyond relationship-driven enterprise sales.

The stock was about **$299.69** on August 24, 2026, with a market capitalization near **$101 billion** and a trailing P/E near **23x**. It has fallen sharply from its recent highs, but remains priced as a premium compounder rather than a normal ad-tech company.

## Latest operating results

Q2 2026 revenue was **$1.924 billion, up 53% year over year**; net income was **$1.267 billion, up 55%**; and adjusted EBITDA was **$1.614 billion, up 58%**, with an 83.9% adjusted EBITDA margin. Net revenue per installation increased 58%, while installation volume declined 2%.

Q3 guidance is revenue of **$2.055–$2.085 billion** and adjusted EBITDA of **$1.710–$1.740 billion**, implying roughly 7% sequential revenue growth at the midpoint and an approximately 83% EBITDA margin. Q2 landed within the prior guidance range, but the market wanted a larger upside surprise and stronger forward momentum.

Cash generation is a major strength: first-half operating cash flow was **$2.16 billion** and reported free cash flow was **$2.15 billion**. The company repurchased about **$1.5 billion** of stock in the first half and had $1.8 billion remaining under its authorization. Balance-sheet cash was $3.05 billion against $3.52 billion of long-term debt.

## Bull thesis

- Axon’s optimization engine continues to raise advertiser ROI, allowing AppLovin to monetize inventory more effectively even when installation volume is flat or falling.
- Opening AppLovin Ads to every advertiser can expand the funnel materially and create a second growth leg outside mobile gaming.
- The business has unusually high margins, strong cash conversion, and a buyback mechanism that can support per-share growth.
- CTV, e-commerce, web advertising, creative automation, and additional advertiser formats offer optionality beyond the current mobile-gaming base.

## Bear thesis

- The valuation still assumes very strong execution. A slowdown from 50%+ growth can compress the multiple quickly, as the post-Q2 reaction demonstrated.
- Installation volume is declining, so future growth depends heavily on continued improvement in monetization and model quality.
- Competition from Google, Meta, TikTok, Unity, and other ad platforms could limit expansion or pressure advertiser economics.
- The company is concentrated in performance advertising and has meaningful exposure to mobile-app budgets, privacy rules, AI regulation, and platform-policy changes.
- Stock-based compensation was $169 million in the first half, so adjusted metrics should be viewed alongside GAAP earnings and dilution.
- AppLovin had a $1.3 billion, three-year minimum cloud-services commitment; $953.5 million had been paid by June 30, 2026. That supports capacity but creates fixed-cost risk if growth weakens.

## Catalysts

1. Evidence that the improved Axon model is lifting Q3 monetization.
2. Self-serve advertiser adoption and measurable traction outside gaming.
3. Continued 50%+ revenue growth, margin durability, and aggressive buybacks.
4. Successful expansion into CTV, e-commerce, web, and creative-generation products.

## What would change the view

**Upgrade:** sustained revenue growth above 40%, stable or rising net revenue per installation, and proof that non-gaming channels are scaling without damaging margins.  
**Downgrade:** two consecutive quarters of decelerating monetization, shrinking advertiser ROI, falling margins, or evidence that self-serve expansion is producing low-quality demand.

## Bottom line

APP is one of the strongest operating businesses in the watchlist, but it is no longer a straightforward momentum buy after the earnings reset. I would classify it as **Medium conviction / accumulate on weakness**, with position sizing below a steadier compounder. Around $300, the risk-reward is materially better than near the highs, but a durable re-rating requires proof that Axon’s performance rebound and broader advertiser rollout are real—not just management promises.
