# AVGO — Broadcom — 2026-08-31

**View:** High conviction business; medium conviction stock at current valuation. **Action:** Hold or accumulate only on pullbacks unless the upcoming earnings report materially raises forward estimates.

**Price / scale:** Approximately $370.34 per share and $1.76T market capitalization as of August 31, 2026. Broadcom combines custom AI accelerators and networking silicon with infrastructure software, including VMware.

**Latest fundamentals:** Fiscal Q2 2026 revenue was $22.187B, up 48% year over year. Semiconductor Solutions revenue rose 79% to $15.009B, while Infrastructure Software grew 9% to $7.178B. AI-semiconductor revenue was $10.8B, up 143%. Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $2.44, adjusted EBITDA was $15.244B (69% of revenue), and free cash flow was $10.262B (46% of revenue). Management guided Q3 revenue to approximately $29.4B, including $16.0B of AI-semiconductor revenue.

**Near-term event:** Q3 FY26 earnings are scheduled for September 2, 2026. The key question is whether the AI ramp, customer commitments, and networking demand support another estimate reset—or whether expectations have already outrun the evidence.

**Bull thesis:** Broadcom is a critical supplier of custom AI accelerators, Ethernet switching, optical connectivity, and related infrastructure. Its small group of hyperscale customers can fund very large programs, while VMware adds recurring software revenue and cross-selling potential. The model is highly cash generative and benefits from operating leverage as AI revenue scales.

**Bear thesis:** The current valuation capitalizes years of exceptional AI growth. Custom silicon programs are concentrated among a few very large customers and can be lumpy, delayed, or insourced. AI infrastructure spending could normalize, competitors could narrow the networking/custom-ASIC advantage, and VMware integration or renewal pressure could slow software growth.

**Risks:** Hyperscaler concentration; dependence on TSMC and other constrained suppliers; semiconductor cyclicality; export controls and trade restrictions; execution risk in very large AI programs; VMware customer churn or pricing resistance; significant acquisition-related debt; dilution and stock-based compensation; and a sharp multiple contraction if AI growth decelerates.

**Catalysts / proof points:** Q3 revenue and AI-semiconductor revenue above guidance; evidence of durable 2027 customer commitments; sustained software ARR growth; stable ~67% non-GAAP operating margin; strong free-cash-flow conversion; and disciplined capital allocation. The most important proof point is whether AI growth broadens beyond a handful of customers while software remains a dependable cash engine.

**Rating:** Medium conviction / hold or accumulate on meaningful weakness. Broadcom is one of the strongest AI-infrastructure compounders, but the stock requires continued beats and raises. Size below a diversified core holding and avoid chasing immediately ahead of earnings.
