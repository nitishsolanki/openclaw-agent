# OKLO — Oklo

## Overview
Oklo is an advanced nuclear company developing compact fast-reactor powerhouses intended to provide clean, reliable power, with additional strategic interest around recycling nuclear fuel. It is closely associated with the AI/data-center power theme and is known for high-profile backing, including Sam Altman involvement.

## Sector / Industry
- Sector: Utilities / Energy Technology
- Industry: Advanced Nuclear / Small Modular Reactors

- Public narrative remains centered on advanced nuclear deployment, regulatory progress, government support, and potential power demand from AI infrastructure.
- SEC context: U.S.-listed issuer; monitor 10-Q/10-K/8-K filings for cash burn, licensing progress, site/customer agreements, and related-party/financing disclosures.

## Bull thesis
- If advanced nuclear gains political and regulatory support, Oklo could be an early public-market beneficiary.
- Data centers and industrial customers increasingly need firm clean power, creating a large theoretical market.
- Government support, fuel-cycle capabilities, or credible customer agreements could validate the model.

## Bear thesis
- Oklo is pre-commercial and depends on difficult regulatory, engineering, financing, and construction milestones.
- Losses and cash burn are expected before revenue scale, increasing dilution risk.
- The stock can trade more on nuclear/AI enthusiasm than fundamental progress.

## Risks
- NRC approval delays; technology execution; financing and dilution; siting/fuel constraints; political shifts; long commercialization timelines; headline volatility.

## Catalysts
- Regulatory milestones; government grants or policy support; customer power agreements; site selection; strategic partnerships; clearer first-reactor timeline.

## Long-term outlook
OKLO is a high-upside, high-uncertainty advanced nuclear option. The opportunity is large, but investors need tolerance for binary regulatory and financing outcomes.

## Conviction rating
Low-Medium conviction.
