# Macro Snapshot — 2026-08-31

- Macro regime: risk-off tilt / neutral. Growth remains resilient, but inflation is elevated and policy uncertainty is rising.
- Fed Chair Kevin Warsh signaled that rate increases may be needed if inflation does not move clearly toward 2%; the next FOMC meeting is September 15–16.
- July PCE inflation remained elevated; the June Fed projections showed 2026 PCE inflation at 3.6%, core PCE at 3.3%, and unemployment at 4.3%.
- Key cross-asset signal: oil shock plus hawkish Fed risk is negative for high-beta software, fintech, crypto-linked, and unprofitable growth; supportive for defense and selected energy/power infrastructure.
